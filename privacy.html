Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

1. Overview

Baseball Fan Notebook is an app for organizing baseball leagues, teams, players, ballparks, game visits, and personal notes. This policy explains how the app handles information you create, import, export, or access through external services.

2. Information the app handles

The app primarily handles content that you create, edit, import, or manage yourself, which may include:

  • League, team, player, and ballpark information
  • Game visit dates, scores, game notes, and calendar notes
  • Trip-planning details such as transportation notes, stay options, and related links
  • JSON backup files that you manually import or export

3. How data is stored

Based on the current product design, core data is stored locally on your device for your personal use and review. The app also provides backup features so you can export data as JSON files and import it again when needed.

4. Backups, import, and export

When you use export features, the app creates JSON backup files containing your data. When you use import features, the selected backup file is read in order to restore or update your records. Where those files are saved and how they are managed afterward depends on the device or file location you choose.

5. Maps and external websites

The app may let you open maps, ticketing pages, team websites, or other external links. When you choose to open those services, the relevant third-party provider may collect technical information or usage data under its own policies. Those third-party practices are not governed by this policy.

6. Analytics, ads, and accounts

Based on the current reference version, the app is not primarily designed around public social accounts, built-in advertising systems, or social feed features. If future versions add sign-in, cloud sync, analytics, or advertising functionality, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.

7. Your controls

You can manage the records and backup files you create through the app's edit, delete, export, and import features.

8. Children's privacy

This app is not intended to collect children's personal information. If you believe inappropriate information has been entered or stored, please remove that content promptly.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be updated due to feature changes, legal requirements, or product adjustments. Updated versions will be posted on this page, and the date shown above will indicate the latest revision.

10. Contact

If you plan to publish this app on the App Store or a public website, you should add a real contact email address or support page here for privacy-related questions.
