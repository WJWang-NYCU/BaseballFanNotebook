Core features

Core features

01

League and team organization

Track MLB or other leagues and structure teams, divisions, and home ballpark information clearly.

02

Ballpark and travel planning

Save ballpark locations, transportation notes, wishlist visits, stay options, and related travel links.

03

Players and favorites

Follow favorite players, jersey numbers, positions, birthplace details, and personal notes in one list.

04

Visits and calendar notes

Record ballpark visits, scores, matchups, and daily notes so each game experience stays complete.

05

JSON export and import

Export your data as JSON backups and restore it later, reducing the risk of losing your personal archive.

06

Built for personal tracking

Designed around personal records, collections, and review rather than public social feeds or rankings.
